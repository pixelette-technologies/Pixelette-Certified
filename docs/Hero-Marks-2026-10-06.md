# Homepage hero: implementation record

The homepage hero diagram is redrawn, 6 October 2026. Customers, regulators and partners no longer trace three routes through controls, governance and evidence. The field is now an orbit of the requirements the firm prepares organisations for.

- **Commit:** `2d4004e` on `main`
- **Status:** implemented and pushed. Deployment still gated by the prerequisites in [[Strategic-Rebuild-Implementation-2026-09-25#Before this can go live]].

The approved headline is unchanged. No public pricing was introduced.

---

## What it shows

Eight original marks, not official scheme artwork. ISO, AICPA, NCSC and PCI SSC control those logos, and displaying them would imply a licence the firm does not hold.

| Mark | Opens |
|---|---|
| ISO 27001 | `/services/iso-27001` |
| ISO 42001 | `/services/iso-42001` |
| SOC 2 | `/services/soc-2` |
| PCI DSS | `/contact` |
| ISO 27701 | `/services/privacy` |
| ISO 22301 | `/services/iso-22301` |
| Cyber Essentials | `/services/cyber-essentials` |
| Cyber Essentials Plus | `/services/cyber-essentials` |

Hovering or focusing a mark pauses the orbit and names it in the centre. The orbit does not run for reduced motion, or on a device without hover. The caption states that assurance is awarded independently. Practitioner credentials, and frameworks that are not certificates, are not drawn as marks.
