# Markets header: implementation record

The coastline map on the markets index is replaced, 6 October 2026. International cannot be drawn as a fourth coastline, so the header is a scope instrument. The ring is international scope. The United Kingdom, the United States and the GCC are routes inside it. Each route, including International, has a line from the centre to a mark beside its name.

- **Commit:** `30a78ae` on `main`
- **Status:** implemented and pushed. Deployment still gated by the prerequisites in [[Strategic-Rebuild-Implementation-2026-09-25#Before this can go live]].

The approved headline is unchanged. No public pricing was introduced. The homepage market switcher is unchanged: International there still shows the wider drawing at an even tone.

---

## What it does

Selecting a name holds that route. Otherwise the instrument moves through the four. A signal travels the selected line, and the field fills when International is selected. With reduced motion, the instrument stays still until a route is chosen.

The lines are routes through the site, not offices, and not a limit on where support is available.
