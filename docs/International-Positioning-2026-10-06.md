# International positioning: implementation record

Public geography restated, 6 October 2026. Pixelette Certified supports organisations internationally. The United Kingdom, the United States and the GCC remain routes through the site because their requirements differ. They are not described as the deepest, strongest or only places the business works, and the site does not claim regulatory expertise in every jurisdiction.

- **Commit:** `1624eca` on `main`
- **Status:** implemented and pushed. Deployment still gated by the prerequisites in [[Strategic-Rebuild-Implementation-2026-09-25#Before this can go live]].

The approved headline is unchanged: "Build the assurance your customers, regulators and partners expect". No public pricing was introduced.
