Same bones, different house

The revamp made Pixelette Certified render as a faithful copy of the group pattern guide, which was drawn around Pixelette Technologies. This page proposes the layer that keeps the shared skeleton intact while giving Certified its own identity, and is the method we will reuse for the rest of the group.

01 The two systems

Hue is not where the difference lives. The guide green sits at 173°, Certified’s teal at 180° and its petrol at 182° — barely nine degrees across the whole set. Swapping one green for another would read as the same site with a fault in it.

What separates the two companies is value structure. Technologies is a one-tone system: a single mid-green at 23% lightness does every job, from eyebrow to button fill to card border. Certified becomes a two-tone system: a dark petrol anchor carrying everything that has to be read, and a bright teal signal that marks and never speaks.

That is also why the bright teal cannot simply take over. At 2.86:1 on white it fails every text threshold, across 61 text-brand call sites plus every filled button. Demoting it to a signal role is what makes it usable at all.

The mark was already right. logo-green-text.svg and logo-white-text.svg have always been #044143 and #14A4A9. Only the stylesheet and the favicon drifted to the guide green. This proposal brings the site back into agreement with its own wordmark rather than inventing a palette.

Everything below stays identical across every Pixelette site. This is what “similar” concretely means:

      05 Signature device

      Sibling brands usually differ by one memorable mannerism. The guide already has one: a 3px brand border across the top of a feature card. Certified needs its own, built from primitives the guide already contains rather than new vocabulary. Switch between all three in the bar and watch the cards in sections 03 and 04.

      Left accent — recommended
      3px signal-teal left border on feature cards; section rules gain a 40px cap aligned to the container. Reads from across the room.
      Eyebrow rule
      A 16px hairline before every eyebrow. Quieter, and reads as a clause marker, which suits an accreditation body.
      Top border
      The guide’s own treatment, unchanged. The control — pick this and Certified has no device of its own.

      06 Display face

      Newsreader Light is the guide’s loudest personality signal. Changing the family while keeping the role — light editorial serif, 300 and 400, identical clamp sizes — would make Certified unmistakable without moving a single measurement. Selecting a face in the bar also reskins the Certified side of sections 03 and 04.

      If a face other than Newsreader wins: the sub-768px rule in globals.css raises display weight from 300 to 400 because Newsreader Light shreds below 30px. Spectral and Source Serif hold their light weight better, so that bump needs re-tuning rather than copying. Narrow this window past 767px to see it fire.
